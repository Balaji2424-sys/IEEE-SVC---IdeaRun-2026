Overview

Power a village.
Empower a community.

IEEE Smart Village empowers underserved communities around the world through local enterprises that generate income as fees for goods and services that uplift their members. Despite continued development efforts, many rural communities across South Asia remain underserved - and the advent of accessible technology can meaningfully address these rural development challenges.

ISV SARC invites young minds - university and college students - to propose ideas for the productive use of technology to serve the underserved through direct community engagement. Every proposal should address the theme and lead to a potential livelihood opportunity.

Electricity

Reliable power as the foundation for opportunity.

Education

Skills and knowledge that compound over time.

Enterprise

Local, self-sustaining income for communities.

Theme 2026

Three pillars, one livelihood

Proposals should weave together all three pillars into a single, community-centred, sustainable solution.

Electricity

Micro-grids, solar solutions, storage and metering that make power reliable enough to build a business on.

  • · Decentralised & renewable generation
  • · Efficient appliances for productive use
  • · Smart metering & load management

Education

Digital literacy, vocational skilling and knowledge transfer that lets communities operate and maintain their own solutions.

  • · Skill-building for local operators
  • · Digital & financial literacy
  • · Youth and women-focused training

Enterprise

Locally-owned, fee-for-service business models that turn power and skills into sustained household income.

  • · Fee-for-service business models
  • · Local ownership & governance
  • · Market linkages for produce & goods

Team Eligibility

Who can take part

IdeaRun is open to university and college students across South Asia. We encourage teams that bring together different disciplines - engineering, design, agriculture, business and the social sciences all have a role to play in community-centred solutions.

Teams spanning multiple disciplines are strongly encouraged.

Guidelines

What your proposal needs

Keep the full proposal to 1000 words or fewer. Judges weigh innovation, sustainability, community engagement and livelihood potential above length or polish.

Evaluation focus

Innovation Sustainability Community Engagement Livelihoods

Awards

Selected ideas receive IdeaRun 2026 cash awards, travel support to present at the ISV Symposium in Kochi (February 2027), and eligibility for 2027 ISV Student Project Grants.

1

Title, Team & Mentor
